    About this tour

    Twelve places, from Giza (c. 2600 BC) to Kigali and Lagos, showing the continent's long record of building cities, universities and monuments. Every stop lists its source and the date it was checked.

    • Virtual tour only. Camera flights are not walking routes, travel times or accessibility guides. Free flight is a steerable point: no lift, drag, bank or collision.
    • 3D scans are © Google; their attributions appear on the globe and must stay visible. Scan dates and detail vary by place, so newer buildings may be missing.
    • No scans? Without a Cesium ion token (or Google key) in config.js the globe falls back to a plain grid.
    • Keys: ← → change stop in the tour; ← → / A D turn in free flight.
